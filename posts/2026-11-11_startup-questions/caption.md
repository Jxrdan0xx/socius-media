Five questions to ask before you join a startup. Save this for your next final round. 📌

1. How long is your runway? In months, not "we're well funded".
2. Why is this role open now? New or backfill, a clear answer matters more than which.
3. What does success look like at 90 days?
4. Who will I work with most? Ask to meet them.
5. What is the equity really worth? Get options, total shares, strike price, vesting and exercise window in writing.

A good startup will be glad you asked. A great one will have the answers ready.

# LinkedIn (Wed 11 Nov 2026, 12:15, Buffer document carousel)

Joining a startup is a bet on the company as much as the role.

The best candidates we work with treat the final round as their interview of the company too. These are the five questions we'd want answered before signing.

1. How long is your runway? In months, and what happens if the next raise slips.

2. Why is this role open now? New or backfill, either is fine. A vague answer isn't.

3. What does success look like at 90 days? If nobody can describe it, the role isn't defined yet. That can be an opportunity, as long as you know it going in.

4. Who will I work with most? Ask to meet them before you sign.

5. What is the equity really worth? A percentage tells you little. Ask for the number of options, total shares, strike price, vesting and cliff, and the exercise window, in writing.

Founders: if your team can answer all five clearly, say so early. It builds trust fast.
