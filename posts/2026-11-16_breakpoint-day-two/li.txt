Day two at Solana Breakpoint, London.

Breakpoint has come to London this year, and our founder Jordan is at Olympia until Tuesday. A few pictures so far: the view over the main hall from the balcony, the station ticket gates in Breakpoint purple, and a bull in sunglasses.

Events like this are a reminder of why we work the way we do. The Solana ecosystem is full of small, serious teams building fast, and the people they need are often in the same room, just not yet in the same conversation. Meeting face to face is still the quickest way to understand what a team is really building and who would thrive there.

At Breakpoint tomorrow? Let's grab a coffee. Hiring for your Solana team, or weighing up your next move in Web3? Send us a message.
