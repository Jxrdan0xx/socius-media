# X only: first protocol engineer thread (27 Oct 2026, 16:30 Europe/London)

## Post 1
Hiring your first smart contract engineer? The mistake we see most: hiring for the language, not the judgement. Plenty can write Solidity or Rust. Far fewer know when not to ship, and what an attacker would try first.

## Post 2
Second: treating security as a later problem. Your first protocol hire sets the habits for tests, reviews and audit readiness. Ask how they've prepared code for an audit, and what they changed after one.

## Post 3
Third: hunting for a unicorn who does contracts, backend and frontend. Decide what the next six months really need and hire for that.
