The best candidates are interviewing you as much as you're interviewing them.

Strong engineers and operators in Web3 and AI usually have options. Before they say yes to a startup, they want clear answers to four things.

Runway: how long the money lasts, and what the plan is after that.

The founders: who they'll learn from, and how decisions actually get made.

Ownership: what they'll build and own, not just what they'll maintain.

The offer: equity they can understand, and a process that respects their time.

If you can answer these openly in the first conversation, you'll close more of the people you want.

Weighing an offer yourself? Ask these four before you sign.
