When should a startup bring in a recruiter? 🤔

Not always, and not on day one. Your first hires often come from your network, and if they can, they should.

It changes when the network runs dry: a role nobody in-house can assess, founders screening CVs instead of building, or a search for people who aren't looking.

Before you call anyone, get the brief straight. Salary range, equity terms, 90-day outcomes, the interview loop, who decides.

Then look for a partner, not a CV feed. Save this for when you're next hiring. 📌
