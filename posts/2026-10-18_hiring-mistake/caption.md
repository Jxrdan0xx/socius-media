The most expensive hiring mistake we see in Web3 startups isn't overpaying.

It's hiring for the logo instead of the stage.

A great CV from a big exchange or an L1 foundation tells you someone can operate inside a well-resourced machine. It doesn't tell you they can build from zero with three people, no playbook and a shifting roadmap.

Before your next senior hire, ask:
1. Have they built at your stage before?
2. What did they personally ship and own end to end?
3. Do they already know the ecosystem your product lives in?

Stage fit beats pedigree, every time.
