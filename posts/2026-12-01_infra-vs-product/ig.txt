Infra or product: which Web3 engineer are you actually hiring? 🛠️

Both write code. Both ship. They solve very different problems.

Infrastructure engineers build the layer everything runs on: nodes, RPC, indexers, validators, networking. They think in systems, uptime and failure modes.

Product engineers build what users touch: wallets, dApps, dashboards, and the wiring between the frontend and on-chain logic. They think in users and speed of iteration.

The easiest way to tell which you need? Ask what's actually breaking. Downtime and slow calls point one way. Users dropping off points the other.

The costly mistake is one job spec for both. Write it around the problem, not the buzzwords. Save this for your next hire. 📌
