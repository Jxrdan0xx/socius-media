Infra or product: which Web3 engineer are you actually hiring?

Both write code. Both ship. They solve very different problems, and mixing them up is one of the most common hiring mistakes we see. 🧵
---
Infrastructure builds the layer everything runs on: nodes, RPC, indexers, validators, networking.

Usually Rust, Go or C++. Thinks in systems, uptime and failure modes.
---
Product builds what users touch: wallets, dApps, dashboards, the wiring to on-chain logic.

Usually TypeScript and React, sometimes Solidity. Thinks in users and shipping speed.
---
Quick test: ask what's actually breaking.

Downtime, slow calls, bad data → infra.
Users dropping off, features stuck in the backlog → product.

Then write the spec around that problem, not the buzzwords.
