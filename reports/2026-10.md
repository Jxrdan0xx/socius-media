## Top 3 and bottom 3 (judged on shares, saves, comments, clicks and follows more than likes)

**Top 3**
1. **On the ground at Token2049 (IG, photo):** 3.7x the reach of any other managed post, the only managed post with comments (2) and the only one to win a follower. A real face, a real event, and a clear next step (Breakpoint). This matches the competitor lesson already in BRAND.md: photos carry the trust.
2. **Three signs you need a specialist recruiter (LinkedIn document):** best LinkedIn post: 75 clicks, 3 comments, 2 shares, 22% engagement. A specific, self-diagnosing hiring-intelligence topic ("how many sound familiar?") that founders recognise themselves in, in a swipeable format.
3. **The hiring mistake costing Web3 startups (LinkedIn document + IG):** 54 clicks and 2 shares on LinkedIn, and second-best IG reach of the graphic posts. Contrarian hook ("it isn't overpaying") plus a 3-question checklist.

**Bottom 3**
1. **Roles worth knowing about (24/26 Oct):** 3 LinkedIn impressions; on IG it had to be reposted manually (19:08) and got 23 reach with no saves or shares. Jordan's own round-up posts on 2 and 15 Oct reached 488 and 665, so role content can work. The difference may be that those were in Jordan's voice with real news (closed roles) and were reshared, but that is a guess.
2. **Before they say yes (light static):** lowest IG reach (16). On LinkedIn it had the most impressions of the month (617) but 0 reactions. People saw it and kept scrolling: a single image with a list doesn't give anything to swipe or answer.
3. **Settle this: remote or in the room (frame static poll):** no comments on IG, LinkedIn or (as far as we know) X. Asking for A/B/C from a ~80-follower account without anyone seeding the first answer didn't start a conversation.

## Patterns
- **Themes:** photo was far ahead (89 reach). Among graphics there was no meaningful difference: midnight 24, white 23, frame 21, gradient 19, light 16. Terminal has no data yet.
- **Pillars:** hiring intelligence drove LinkedIn (both top posts). Conversation (poll) and candidate value (static) were weakest on both channels. Proof/brand via a real photo was the Instagram winner.
- **Slide types / format:** LinkedIn document carousels got 54–75 clicks and ~21–22% engagement vs 15–18 clicks and 2–5% for single images. On Instagram, carousel vs static made little difference apart from the photo post.
- **Posting times:** both 12:00 Instagram posts had the lowest reach (16, 21); evening posts got 19–89. This overlaps with format (both noon posts were statics), so it's weak evidence. LinkedIn 08:45 vs 12:15 is inconclusive.
- **Non-follower reach:** not available from the tools.
- **Saves and shares:** zero on every managed Instagram post. That's the biggest gap.
- **Channels:** LinkedIn is the only channel producing meaningful interaction (1,703 impressions, 162 clicks, 5 shares from 5 posts), which fits its top-priority status. Instagram is small (79 followers) and only lifts with people content. X can't be assessed.

## What we changed (todo posts only; nothing already scheduled was touched)
1. **LinkedIn defaults to document carousels.** Added a `format_review` note to `config.linkedin`. The 13 Nov Breakpoint post moved from static to a 4-slide carousel (event card, why we meet in person, who we'd like to meet, CTA).
2. **No more noon Instagram slots.** 21 Nov Meet Josh moved 12:00 → 18:00; December posts on 1 and 16 Dec moved to 18:00, and 7 and 19 Dec to 20:00. RUNBOOK rotation is now 18:00 / 20:00.
3. **New approach for roles and tips posts.** 9 Nov live roles: 3–4 roles, one per slide, no counts, LinkedIn on Monday as a document with links in the first comment only. 11 Nov "Five questions": built as a keepable checklist to test whether we can earn saves.
4. People/photo posts stay protected (3 Nov photo, 19 Nov Breakpoint recap). Added a short "Performance learnings" section to BRAND.md. **Content mix percentages are unchanged:** one month of ~6 posts isn't enough to move them.
5. Recorded the Facebook disconnection in `config.facebook.status_note`.
